# Right-To-Left (RTL) Creative & Video Specifications (Arabic & Urdu)

Translating English creative into Arabic or Urdu by just replacing words creates jarring, poorly performing ads. Because eye tracking scans right-to-left in RTL languages, the entire visual composition must be horizontally mirrored.

---

## 1. The RTL Eye-Tracking F-Pattern

In English (LTR), the viewer's eye enters at the top-left and sweeps right:
`[Logo / Hook (Top Left)] ---------------> [Action (Right)]`

In Arabic / Urdu (RTL), the viewer's eye enters at the **top-right** and sweeps left:
`[Action (Left)] <--------------- [Logo / Hook (Top Right)]`

### Visual Flipping Rules:
1. **Brand Logo:** Place in top-right or center, never pinned to top-left.
2. **Before / After Grids:** "Before" (The Problem) must be on the **Right**; "After" (The Solution) must be on the **Left**. If reversed, viewers perceive the product as causing the problem!
3. **Motion Direction:** Movement indicating forward progress or speed must flow from right to left.
4. **Call-to-Action Arrows:** Swipe or click arrows must point left (`←` or `👆`), not right (`→`).

---

## 2. Typography & Arabic/Nastaliq Font Standards

- **Avoid Latin Monospace Fonts for Arabic Script:** Direct Google Fonts substitutions often break ligature connections between letters.
- **Recommended Web & Motion Fonts:**
  - Modern Clean: *Cairo*, *Almarai*, *IBM Plex Sans Arabic*
  - Traditional / High Trust: *Amiri*, *Noto Naskh Arabic*
  - Urdu Editorial: *Jameel Noori Nastaleeq*, *Noto Nastaliq Urdu*
- **Line-Height Multiplier:** Arabic script requires 1.4x to 1.6x the line-height of Latin text to prevent diacritics (harakat/aerab) from clipping.
